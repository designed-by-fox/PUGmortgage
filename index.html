About Brian

Clear mortgage guidance in a changing market

Brian Kurtz is a Chicago-based mortgage broker with Pathway Underwriting Group, specializing in first-time homebuyers, veterans, and high-net-worth individuals. Brian brings a background in private mortgage banking, giving clients a level of financial and operational insight that goes beyond a typical loan origination process.

Brian's practice is built around clarity and personal attention: walking first-time buyers through every step of financing, helping veterans navigate VA loan benefits, and structuring more complex lending solutions for high-net-worth clients. He's active across a variety of Chicago's neighborhoods, and also holds a mortgage broker's license in Washington state.
