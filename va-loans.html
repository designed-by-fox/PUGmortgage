Why Veterans Choose VA Loans

One of the most valuable benefits available to those who served

Backed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, the VA loan program gives eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and certain surviving spouses real financial advantages over conventional financing.

01

$0 Down Payment

Most eligible buyers can purchase with no down payment at all, compared to the 5–20% conventional loans typically require.

02

No PMI

VA loans don't require private mortgage insurance, which can save borrowers hundreds of dollars a month.

03

Competitive Rates

Because VA loans are government-backed, they carry less risk for lenders, often resulting in more competitive interest rates.

04

Flexible Qualifying

Credit and debt-to-income requirements tend to be more flexible than conventional guidelines.

05

Capped Closing Costs

The VA caps certain closing costs a lender can charge, and sellers are permitted to contribute toward the rest.

06

Reusable Entitlement

The VA loan benefit isn't a one-time offer. Entitlement can be restored and reused multiple times over a veteran's life.

07

Assumable Loans

In many cases, a VA loan can be assumed by a qualified future buyer, which can be a selling point down the road.

08

No Prepayment Penalty

Pay the loan off early with no added cost, whenever that makes sense for you.
